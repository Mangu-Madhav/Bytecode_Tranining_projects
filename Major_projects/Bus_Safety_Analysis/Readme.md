**🚌 Bus Safety Analysis Dashboard**

**📌 Project Overview**

In 2025, there has been a noticeable increase in bus-related incidents, including accidents and tire bursts.
This project aims to analyze operational and driver-related risk factors contributing to these incidents and provide data-driven solutions to improve road safety.

The dashboard was developed using Power BI to transform raw incident data into actionable safety insights.

**🎯 Problem Statement**

Bus transportation systems are facing increasing safety challenges due to factors such as:

- Driver fatigue

- Night shift frequency

- Long trip durations

- Limited driver experience

- Aging fleet conditions

- The objective of this project is to:

- Identify root causes behind bus-related incidents

- Detect high-risk operational patterns

- Propose predictive and preventive strategies

- Reduce incident rates through data-driven decision-making

**🛠 Tools & Technologies Used**

- Power BI (Dashboard Development & Data Visualization)

- Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

- Risk Pattern Analysis

**🔄 Project Workflow**

**1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing**

- Handled inconsistent and missing values

- Structured raw operational data

- Standardized categorical and numerical fields

**2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**

- Identified fatigue trends

- Analyzed incident distribution patterns

- Examined driver experience and age factors

- Evaluated trip duration impact

- Compared incident types across bus categories

**3. KPI & Risk Pattern Identification**

- Designed key performance indicators such as:

- Total number of incidents

- Average driver experience

- Average driver age

- Average bus age

- Average speed

- Average trip duration

**4. Interactive Dashboard Development**

- Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with:

- Dynamic filtering (Severity of Cause, Breakdown Risk)

- Incident trend visualization

- Driver risk segmentation

- Fleet-based risk analysis

**📊 Key Analytical Findings**

- Increased night shift frequency leads to higher driver fatigue.

- Drivers aged 25–34 exhibit elevated fatigue scores.

- Longer trip durations are associated with higher tire burst incidents.

- Drivers with lower experience levels are involved in more incidents.

- Night trips show higher accident probability.

- Certain bus types have disproportionately high incident counts.

**🚀 Proposed Predictive & Preventive Models**

To move from reactive reporting to proactive prevention, the following models were proposed:

**1. Fatigue Risk Tool**

Predicts whether a driver is likely to become fatigued in the next 4 hours based on:

- Night shift frequency

- Trip duration

- Driver age

- Historical fatigue patterns

**2. Breakdown Predictor**

Identifies buses requiring urgent maintenance before departure by analyzing:

- Bus age

- Incident history

- Breakdown risk level

**3. Automated Driver Swaps**

Recommends safer driver reassignment when fatigue risk exceeds threshold levels.

**4. Smart Repairs**

Prioritizes maintenance scheduling for high-risk buses and frequently failing vehicles.

**📈 Business Impact**

- By implementing the above predictive and preventive strategies, bus-related incidents can potentially be reduced by up to 30%.

- This approach enables organizations to transition from reactive incident management to proactive risk prevention.
